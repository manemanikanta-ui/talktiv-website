Manikanta Mane

Founder & CEO · Masters in Telecommunications

📍 Hyderabad, India · 🎓 Melbourne, Australia

Talktiv began when Manikanta watched his father — a Telugu-speaking real estate agent in Hyderabad — lose clients every week because of language barriers. That frustration became a mission: no Indian business should ever lose a customer because of language.

After completing his Masters in Telecommunications in Melbourne, Manikanta returned to build the voice infrastructure layer for India — combining world-class AI with deep understanding of India's linguistic diversity.
